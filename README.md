## 📱 Responsive User Interface

A user interface should behave like a well-designed tool belt: everything within reach, nothing in the way. The responsive design philosophy documented here ensures that whether you are reviewing a bid from a desktop workstation in the office or checking a schedule update on a laptop at the job trailer, the experience remains comfortable and legible.

Key responsive principles covered:

- **Fluid Layouts** — panels and tables reflow gracefully as window sizes change
- **Touch-Friendly Controls** — buttons sized appropriately for gloved hands on touchscreen devices
- **High-Contrast Modes** — readability under harsh sunlight or dim warehouse lighting
- **Keyboard Navigation** — efficient workflows for power users who prefer shortcuts
- **Scalable Typography** — text that stays crisp on high-DPI displays

## 🏗️ Project Management Reinvented

Project management in construction is a juggling act performed on a moving train. This section of the hub collects methodologies, templates, and mental models for keeping every ball in the air.

### Core Pillars

1. **Planning** — Define scope, milestones, and deliverables before the first shovel hits the dirt.
2. **Scheduling** — Sequence tasks with realistic dependencies and buffer time.
3. **Budgeting** — Track committed costs against estimates in real time.
4. **Communication** — Keep clients, crews, and vendors on the same page.
5. **Documentation** — Capture every decision, approval, and change.

### Metaphor Corner

Think of a construction project as a river. Planning is the riverbed that gives it direction. Scheduling is the current that carries it forward. Budgeting is the banks that keep it from flooding. Communication is the water itself — without it, nothing moves.
